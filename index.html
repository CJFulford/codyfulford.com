My name is Cody Fulford.

I currently live in Calgary, Alberta, Canada where I was born in 1994.

I am currently employed at Integrity Post Structures in Okotoks, Alberta, Canada where I work under Daniel Maher building an industry leading job management program.

In April of 2017, I finished my five-year career at the University of Calgary, graduating with a bachelors degree in computer science, graphics concentration.

Academic Career

When my university studies began I was majoring in archaeology. However, I soon realized that I loved history, but found the act of archaeology far too dry to continue professionally. I branched out, trying to find a new path and eventually enrolled in an introductory programming class. I quickly realized just how much computers can do in a short period of time and became very excited with the possibilities. I changed my major to computer science and eventually added a concentration for computer graphics. After being accepted to the graphics concentration, I felt that I had found my purpose and poured everything I had into my schooling. Dramatically increasing my marks and truly enjoying every one of the classes that I took. In 2017, I graduated university with a degree and a GPA of 3.9 In my final year and 3.6 overall.

Athletic Career

My passion for sports started at Dr. E.P. Scarlett High School in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. The first time I entered the high school I was immediately asked to join the football team by one of the coaches. I found my love of football and of sports in general during the following season. After a relatively successful season, I was one of two recipients out of a team of fifty that received the Lancer Pride Award (see the quote at the top of the page). I strive to give the same level of effort and commitment to every aspect of my life. After graduating high school, I returned to the Scarlet football field as a coach where I became the offensive line coach for 3 years before becoming the offensive coordinator in my fourth year. I have since taken a break from coaching to focus my efforts on my university studies and programming career. I have been skiing since I was two years old and golfing since fourteen. I am a fan of the Calgary Stampeders, Flames, and Seattle Seahawks
